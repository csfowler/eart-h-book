2  Origins

I am C. Ezra Stiles, peripatetic cartographer of the Golden Fountain on Kiliman. I record my thoughts here in the spirit of scholarship that has long motivated my order. Much of what I write is speculation, though such is arguably true of all that we call knowledge. My thanks to all those brave souls who continue to explore, invent, and document. Mistakes are most assuredly my own.

2.1 In the Beginning…

As far as we know the Univers[ity] was always in existence. Our story begins when the entity [Profes]Sor [Fow]Ler had an idea. From the idea and from the power granted to Sor Ler hundreds of worlds were created all of them different but strangely similar.

EARTA and EARTB were the first to emerge and their stories are told elsewhere for now. EARTH, where our story takes place, was among the very first and the care of its design reveals the art of far more than a mere clockmaker. Sor Ler appointed RA to oversee EARTH and in the early days it is said that the entity was ever present; building the mountains, filling the seas, shaping the earliest creatures to roam the world.

Oral histories passed down from generation to generation speak of late nights when the mottled face of RA could almost be perceived in the heavens. Whether these stories are apocryphal or have, within them, a kernel of truth is unknown. What is known is that centuries if not millenia have passed in which EARTH has not known direct action by RA.

Though some still burn offerings to this vanished entity, the world itself has moved on playing out the magnificent experiment that this being orchestrated with no understanding of how or why it continues to run.

2.2 The Great Experiment

The nature of RA’s experiment remains one of the great mysteries of EARTH. Scholars debate whether the complex interplay of the three moons was intentional, whether the distribution of magical energy across the planet serves some greater purpose, or whether EARTH has simply evolved beyond its creator’s original vision. What is not in doubt is that this is an experiment. The landscape is littered with dozens if not hundreds of idiosyncratic places and items that clearly have their origins elsewhere. Some of the places, like those that offer healing, are patterned with such regularity that some plan must be at work, but the nature of that plan remains opaque. Numerous cults have risen and fallen over the centuries purporting to predict the end of the experiment and thus the end of EARTH itself, but so far the experiment continues at its own pace and with its own mysterious logic.

2.3 The Vanishing

Historical records suggest that RA’s presence began to wane approximately 2,000 years ago, though the exact timeline remains disputed. The last confirmed manifestation occurred during what is now called the “Extension,” when several major magical artifacts were created simultaneously across different continents. While most processes on EARTH seem to have proceeded without interruption, there are several parts of the planet where ecosystems or natural processes seem to have gone wild in the absence of direct influence. These places are a focus for both scholars and adventurers because their obvious flaws are thought to shed light on underlying logics of the experiment, while also producing higher than average numbers of inexplicable phenomena.

2.4 Today

EARTH in the year 2026 is a complex and diverse world. It is characterized by chaos more than order with the surges and ebbs of lunar power making long term projects difficult to complete. Societies have adapted to this instability in various ways, with some embracing nomadic lifestyles while others have developed intricate systems of alliances and trade to mitigate the risks associated with power fluctuations. No one civilization dominates the planet, and there are vast reaches that remain lightly settled. Cartographers seeking the wisdom of the healing sites have visited most corners of the world in their quest to unravel the order and disorder of that system, but many remote locations have seen fewer than a dozen visitors in centuries.

This project has emerged from my desire to create a world in which I can explore ideas about power, identity, and social formation. I am increasingly intrigued by the literature on Futurisms, and the claim that emerges from that work that we have difficulty imagining just futures because we are so closely tied to the present and the institutions we already have in place. By creating a world with different rules; particularly related to property rights and racial identity, I hope to be able to explore these ideas in a way that is less bound by my own experiences.

Concurrently, I have become intrigued by the idea of storytelling as a way to teach students about geography and about the topics of population, economy, and institutions that I teach in my classes. I am increasingly aware of the limitations of lectures conducted in front of students who are only partially engaged. I worry about a world in which ideas are simply something that gets looked up or sourced from an AI instead of things that are chewed on, tested, and refined. My hope is that this project can serve as a starting point for a new way of teaching students–through stories and through play. My intent is to use these footnotes moving forward as a place to make connections between the academic literature that motivates both this project and the implementation here. Ultimately, I am not sure whether I am making a game, a book, or a syllabus. Perhaps all three.
